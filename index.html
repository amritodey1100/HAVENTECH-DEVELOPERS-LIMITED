Company History

With pleasure, we are introducing our first project at Sikder Real Estate, West Dhanmondi, one of the most planned residential areas of this metropolis, Haventech Heights.

Located at 295/Ja/3/H Sikder Real Estate, Rayer bazar, Dhaka, this South facing plot is owned by the directors of the company and blessed with excellent road networks, convenient and easily accessible transport facilities.

While designing, architects put most of the efforts on maximization of functional aspects keeping in mind the aesthetic sense. Three beautiful apartments spread on each floor, a symbol of perfect contemporary architecture, equipped with modern and stylish fittings and fixtures.
